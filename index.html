Learn to Read Piano Sheet Music Notes — Free and Online

Piano Note Trainer is an interactive, 100% free tool for practicing sight-reading by identifying musical notes on a real 88-key piano. Train your reading of the treble clef and bass clef, with natural notes, sharps (#) and flats (♭), at your own pace, right in your browser — no install, no account needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is sheet music note reading?

It's the skill of recognizing, just by looking at the staff, which note each notehead on a line or space represents, and finding it directly on the piano without having to count. It's the foundation of sight-reading.

What's the difference between the treble clef and the bass clef?

The treble clef is used for higher notes (usually the right hand) and the bass clef for lower notes (usually the left hand). Together they form the "grand staff" used in piano sheet music.

Do I need a physical piano to practice?

No. The app includes a virtual 88-key piano: you can play it with a click (computer) or a tap (phone or tablet). If you have a real piano or keyboard, you can use it alongside the app too.

Is it really free?

Yes, Piano Note Trainer is 100% free, runs right in your browser, and doesn't require creating an account.
